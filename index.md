# Privacy Policy — HPPin

**Effective date:** 2026-10-16
**Last updated:** 2026-10-16

HPPin ("the App", package `app.hppin.com`) is a cost-of-goods (HPP) and
selling-price calculator for small food and beverage businesses. This Privacy
Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored, and the
choices available to you.

> 🇮🇩 **Versi Bahasa Indonesia tersedia di bagian bawah halaman ini.**

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect personal information about you and does **not**
transmit your data to our servers — we do not operate any backend server.
The App works fully offline and requires no account.

The App stores the following information **locally on your device**:

- **Business data you input:** ingredients and packaging (purchase prices,
  pack sizes, price history), recipes, monthly business costs, sales
  channels and their fees, selling prices, stock counts and stock-take
  records, and your shopping list.
- **Application settings:** your nickname and business name (optional, used
  only for the home-screen greeting), theme, dark mode, and label color.
- **In-app purchase state:** a flag indicating whether you have purchased
  HPPin Pro via Google Play.

All of the above is stored in a local SQLite database within the App's
private storage directory on your device. We have no access to it.

## 2. Third-Party Services

### 2.1 Google Play Billing
When you purchase HPPin Pro, Google Play handles payment processing. We
never see your credit card or payment details. Google Play returns only a
confirmation that the purchase was completed. See
[Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and
the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The App contains **no advertising and no analytics/tracking SDKs**. All
fonts are bundled inside the App; nothing is downloaded at runtime.

## 3. Backups

The App provides a manual "Backup" feature that exports your data to a JSON
file. **You choose** where this file is sent (e.g., your own Google Drive or
WhatsApp) via the Android share menu. The backup file contains your business
data — store it somewhere you trust. We never receive a copy. Restoring a
backup replaces the data on your device after your confirmation.

Android may also back up app data automatically to your Google account,
according to your device's backup settings.

## 4. Data Deletion

Your data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. To delete
all data, clear the App's storage (Android Settings → Apps → HPPin → Clear
data) or uninstall the App. There is no account to close. Backup files you
created are under your own control and should be deleted separately if
desired.

## 5. Permissions

The App requests only the INTERNET and network-state permissions, used
exclusively by Google Play Billing to buy or restore HPPin Pro. It does not
access your contacts, location, camera, SMS, or files outside what you
explicitly pick during restore.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at
children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone,
including children.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this
page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy: **reinhart.juniars@gmail.com**

# Kebijakan Privasi — HPPin (Bahasa Indonesia)

**Tanggal berlaku:** 16-10-2026 · **Terakhir diperbarui:** 16-10-2026

## 1. Informasi yang Kami Kumpulkan

HPPin **tidak mengumpulkan** informasi pribadi Anda dan **tidak mengirim**
data Anda ke server kami — kami tidak mengoperasikan server apa pun.
Aplikasi bekerja sepenuhnya offline dan tanpa akun.

Data berikut disimpan **hanya di perangkat Anda**: bahan dan kemasan (harga
beli, isi kemasan, riwayat harga), resep, biaya bulanan usaha, tempat jualan
dan potongannya, harga jual, stok dan stok opname, daftar belanja, serta
pengaturan aplikasi (nama panggilan, nama usaha, tema, mode gelap). Status
pembelian HPPin Pro disimpan sebagai penanda lokal. Semuanya berada di
database SQLite di penyimpanan privat aplikasi — kami tidak punya akses.

## 2. Layanan Pihak Ketiga

- **Google Play Billing** — memproses pembayaran HPPin Pro. Kami tidak
  pernah melihat detail pembayaran Anda.

Aplikasi **tidak memuat iklan dan tidak memakai SDK analitik/pelacak**.
Semua font sudah ada di dalam aplikasi; tidak ada unduhan saat dipakai.

## 3. Cadangan

Fitur "Cadangkan" mengekspor data Anda ke file JSON, dan **Anda sendiri
yang memilih** tujuannya (mis. Google Drive atau WhatsApp) lewat menu bagikan
Android. Kami tidak pernah menerima salinannya. Memulihkan cadangan akan
mengganti data di perangkat setelah konfirmasi Anda.

## 4. Penghapusan Data

Hapus data aplikasi (Pengaturan Android → Aplikasi → HPPin → Hapus data)
atau copot pemasangan HPPin. Tidak ada akun yang perlu ditutup. File
cadangan yang pernah Anda buat berada dalam kendali Anda sendiri.

## 5. Izin Aplikasi

Hanya izin INTERNET dan status jaringan, dipakai Google Play Billing untuk
membeli atau memulihkan HPPin Pro. Tidak mengakses kontak, lokasi, kamera,
SMS, maupun file selain yang Anda pilih sendiri saat memulihkan cadangan.

## 6. Kontak

Pertanyaan: **reinhart.juniars@gmail.com**
